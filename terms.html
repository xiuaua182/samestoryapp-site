1. What the service is

SameSTORY is a remote shared-reading app: an adult (the Reader) reads a story aloud while the child (the Sonhador) follows the synchronized illustrations on another device. The app offers a catalog of original children's stories, device pairing, real-time synchronization, and — on the Premium plan — a live voice call during reading.

The application is operated by Gustavo Silva ("we"), available on the Google Play store under the identifier com.samestoryapp.app.

The child does not create an account or sign in. The child uses the app only through a device that the responsible adult deliberately linked (paired) using a code or QR Code generated in the adult's account. By pairing a device for a child, you declare that you are the child's legal guardian — or that you have the legal guardian's authorization — and that you authorize that child's use of the app under our Privacy Policy.
