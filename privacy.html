Na Mesma Estória · SameSTORY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 05/30/2026

SameSTORY (“Na Mesma Estória”) is an app that brings families closer across distance: an adult reads a story aloud while the child follows the synchronized illustrations on a separate device. This policy explains, in plain terms, what data the app collects, why, and what your rights are.

We take privacy seriously — especially because this is an app used by families with children. We show no ads and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

The SameSTORY (Na Mesma Estória) app is operated by Gustavo Silva, who is responsible for processing your data (the “controller”).

For anything related to privacy, contact us at samestoryapp@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. Most information is tied to an account (which may be anonymous).

CategoryWhat it is
Account & identityYou can use the app anonymously (no sign-up) or create an account via email or your Google account. If you sign up, we store your email and, optionally, a name. If you choose to provide the child’s name, it is used only to personalize the experience.
Device pairingTo link the adult’s device to the child’s, we generate a temporary code and store a reference to the paired devices (device nickname, dates, and connection status).

Real-time reading sessionWhile you read together, we sync the current page and reactions (emojis) between the two devices. This is temporary session data.
NotificationsA device notification identifier (token) and the time window you set for receiving alerts.

Technical dataBasic information generated automatically by the underlying services, such as an app installation identifier, device type, and language.

3. Audio: we do not record

The voice call (an optional feature) connects the adult’s microphone to the child’s device live, in real time, like a baby monitor. This audio is transmitted directly between devices through our voice provider and is not recorded, not stored, and not listened to by us. When the call ends, no copy of what was said remains.

4. How we use the data

  • Enable shared, synchronized reading between devices;
  • Keep your account, history, and favorite stories;
  • Send the “your Sonhador wants to read with you” notification within the time window you choose;
  • Enable the live voice call during reading;
  • Understand usage in an aggregated, anonymous way to fix issues and improve the app;
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable.

5. Who we share with

We do not sell your data. We share the minimum necessary with technology providers that run the app’s infrastructure, under their privacy terms:

  • Google (Firebase) — account authentication, database, real-time sync, notifications, and analytics. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Agora — transport for the live voice call (no recording). See agora.io/privacy-policy.
  • Google Play — app distribution and related services.

These providers may process data outside your country. We use services that provide adequate safeguards for international transfers.

6. Children

The app is designed to be set up and managed by a responsible adult, who creates the account and pairs the child’s device. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children beyond what is needed for shared reading (such as session state and device pairing).

We display no advertising, run no personalized ads, and keep analytics in anonymous mode with the advertising identifier disabled. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have collected a child’s data without proper authorization, contact us so we can remove it.

7. Device permissions

  • Microphone — used only during the live voice call, when you start it. Without a call, the microphone is not accessed.
  • Notifications — to alert the adult when the child wants to read together.
  • Camera — only if you choose to scan a pairing QR code.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while the account exists. Pairing codes are temporary (they expire within minutes). Real-time session data is ephemeral. When you delete your account (see section 11), we remove the associated data, except what we must keep due to legal obligations.

9. Security

We use recognized providers with encrypted transmission and access rules that restrict each account to its own data. No system is 100% immune, but we work to protect your information.

10. Your rights

Under Brazil’s LGPD (and equivalent laws such as the GDPR, where applicable), you may request: confirmation of and access to your data; correction of incomplete or outdated data; anonymization or deletion; portability; and information about sharing. To exercise any right, write to contato@samestoryapp.com.

11. Account & data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing samestoryapp@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”. We will handle the request within a reasonable timeframe. Memories saved locally on the device are erased when the app is uninstalled.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or in the law. When that happens, we update the “last updated” date at the top. Material changes may be communicated within the app.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at samestoryapp@gmail.com.
